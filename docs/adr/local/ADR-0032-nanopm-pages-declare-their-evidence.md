# nanopm pages declare their evidence

## Status

Accepted

## Context

[product/ADR-0004](../product/ADR-0004-evidence.md) asks every product
artifact for an `evidence:` key. Since
[ADR-0031](ADR-0031-product-artifacts-live-in-the-nanopm-wiki.md), product
artifacts live in `.nanopm/wiki/`, and nanopm 0.26.0 does not write that key.
What it writes depends on the page:

- **Opportunities**, `entities/opportunities/*.md`, are users' problems.
  Their frontmatter carries `provenance:`, which says where the problem
  came from: `evidence-backed`, `user-stated` or `nano-hypothesis`.
- **Pages of `docs/`** written by most skills (strategy, objectives,
  personas…) carry the same `provenance:` key.
- **PRDs**, `docs/prds/*.md`, written by `pm-prd`, have no frontmatter at
  all, and they are the files the handoff gate of
  [product/ADR-0005](../product/ADR-0005-handoff.md) reads.

`index.md` and `log.md` are generated by nanopm on every write. They are
not artifacts, and `.gitignore` leaves them out.

## Decision

**A page that carries `provenance:` gets `evidence:` from it**, written
next to it when the page is created or its provenance changes:

| `provenance:` | `evidence:` | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `evidence-backed` | `observed` | seen in feedback, interviews or data |
| `user-stated` | `hypothesis` | what the maintainer says users need has not been seen in use |
| `nano-hypothesis` | `hypothesis` | the agent's guess |

No provenance gives `validated`: that level is reached only by a test, as
ADR-0004 says.

**A PRD gets a frontmatter block when `pm-prd` writes it**, with
`artifact: prd` and `evidence:`. Its level is at most the level of its
parent opportunity, found through the `opportunity:` key of the solution
it specifies. The opportunity's level is a ceiling, not a proof: the
solution's own assumptions are not tested by the problem being real.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The handoff gate keeps its meaning: a change built on a PRD whose
  problem was never observed has to declare a `Probe:`.
- The level follows from a key nanopm already writes, so it is not a
  second judgement on the same page.

### Negative

- Nothing writes `evidence:` but the agent or the maintainer; until it is
  added, the ADR-0004 check fails on the page.
- A PRD's frontmatter is added after nanopm writes it, and a later
  `pm-prd` run on the same feature may drop it.
- If nanopm adds or renames a provenance, the table has to follow.

### Neutral

- The 12 artifacts under `.nanopm/wiki/research/`, written with pm-skills,
  already declare `evidence:` and are not concerned.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Narrow the root to `.nanopm/wiki/research/`.** The check passes today,
  and everything nanopm writes escapes the gates.
- **`user-stated` as `observed`.** Takes the maintainer's word for what
  users do. The readiness assessment of 2026-11-21 rated that knowledge
  1-4 of 10.
- **The PRD takes the level of its own sources.** `pm-prd` cites personas,
  feedback and data pages, but the problem it solves is the opportunity's.
